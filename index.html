Managerial Roadmap

Strategic Recommendations & Analytical Limitations

Actionable Recommendations

1. Shift Rs. 125K from Facebook to SMS: Reallocate 40% of Facebook spend to SMS retention and in-store merchandising (projected +Rs. 1.18M revenue lift).

2. Omnichannel QR Bridges: Capture retail in-store footfall into SMS/Email loyalty lists with an instant 5% POS coupon.

3. Mid-Year Focus: Concentrate major product launches and discount budgets during June–July peak demand.

4. ROAS Guardrails: Automatically pause any digital campaign that drops below 4.0x ROAS after Rs. 5,000 spend.

Analytical Limitations (Viva Defense)

• Sample Size: 148 campaigns across 9 months (excludes Q4 holiday peak period).

• Revenue vs. Profit: Lack of Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) means high-revenue discounts may carry lower net margins.

• Single-Touch Attribution: Multi-touch omnichannel customer journeys are aggregated to a primary campaign identifier.

Viva Examiner High-Scoring Cheat Sheet

Q1: Why is Revenue Mean (Rs. 124K) double the Median (Rs. 63K)?

Revenue exhibits extreme positive skewness caused by blockbuster In-Store promotions (e.g. PC0003: Rs. 954K, PC0002: Rs. 788K). The median is a more representative baseline for standard operational expectations.

Q2: How did you safeguard against ZeroDivisionError in Python?

Using numpy.where(df['Spend'] > 0, df['Revenue'] / df['Spend'], 0.0) for ROAS, CTR, and Conversion Rate. This guarantees vector safety without throwing runtime exceptions.

Q3: Why impute missing Spend with Median instead of Mean?

The arithmetic mean is pulled upwards by outlier campaigns. Median (Rs. 13,535.24) represents the true 50th percentile of typical campaign budgets without introducing artificial inflation.

Q4: What is the primary commercial recommendation?

Reallocate 40% of Facebook's budget (~Rs. 125K) into high-performing SMS (12.68x ROAS) and physical In-Store visual merchandising (27.84x ROAS) for an estimated +Rs. 1.18M net revenue lift.

Formal Academic Submission

Project Report: Evaluating Promotional Campaign Performance

BrightWave Retail • Course: BBA Semester III — Python for Data Analytics for Business (Indian Market)

1. Executive Summary

BrightWave Retail executed 148 verified marketing campaigns across five channels and five promotional types between January and September 2026. Across the portfolio, BrightWave invested Rs. 2,045,582.47 in promotional spend, generating Rs. 18,452,391.82 in total revenue, delivering an overall portfolio ROAS of 9.02x. In-Store promotions drove 57.1% of revenue with a 27.84x ROAS, while Facebook underperformed severely at 3.25x ROAS.

2. Objectives

  • Clean and harmonize 150 historical records, eliminating duplicates and standardizing text.
  • Engineer standardized funnel metrics: Click-Through Rate (CTR), Conversion Rate (CVR), and ROAS.
  • Evaluate cross-channel and seasonal performance dynamics using descriptive and visual analytics.
  • Formulate evidence-based budget reallocations to improve return on investment.

3. Dataset Description & Hygiene

The unit of observation is an individual promotional campaign. The raw dataset contained 150 rows. Two duplicate rows were dropped (PC0019 and PC0065). Missing numerical fields (Spend and Leads) were imputed using medians (Rs. 13,535.24 and 298 leads respectively). Categorical inconsistencies were resolved across Channel, Campaign Type, Region, and Product Category.

4. Four Core Strategic Findings

1. Physical Retail Foot-Traffic Fortress

In-Store produced Rs. 10.54M (57.1% of all revenue) at 27.84x average ROAS.

2. The Facebook Budget Drain

Absorbed Rs. 312K in spend but delivered only Rs. 789K in revenue (3.25x ROAS, 2.21% CTR).

3. Direct-to-Consumer Efficiency

SMS (12.68x ROAS) and Email (10.15x ROAS) demonstrate high conversion intent.

4. Summer Peak Seasonality

July (Rs. 3.13M) and June (Rs. 2.85M) generated over 32% of total nine-month revenue.

5. Strategic Business Recommendations

1. Reallocate Rs. 125,000 from Facebook to SMS & In-Store: Shift capital from underperforming paid social into high-converting first-party CRM and physical POS displays.
2. Omnichannel QR Flywheel: Capture in-store retail footfall into SMS/Email retention flows with instant POS coupon codes.
3. Synchronized Mid-Year Campaign Blitz: Heavy-up promotion budgets during June and July to maximize peak seasonal consumer demand.
4. Automated ROAS Guardrails: Kill any digital campaign that drops below 4.0x ROAS after Rs. 5,000 in spend.
